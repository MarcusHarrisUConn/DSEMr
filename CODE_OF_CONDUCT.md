# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DSEMr a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be respectful, accept constructive methodological review,
document uncertainty, avoid overstating software capabilities, and focus
criticism on ideas and evidence. Harassment, personal attacks, publication of
private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting
are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the package maintainer. The maintainer may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is inappropriate. This policy follows the spirit of
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
